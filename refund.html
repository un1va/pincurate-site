Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

We want you to be happy with PinCurate Pro. If it is not right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, no questions asked. This applies to the monthly, yearly and Lifetime plans.

How to ask for a refund

Email univa.apps@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout or your order number. Refunds are issued through Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, to the original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Renewals

  • You can cancel at any time with the link in your purchase email. If you cancel at the end of the billing period, Pro stays active until then; if you cancel immediately, Pro ends right away.
  • If a renewal charged you by surprise, write to us within 7 days of the renewal and we will refund it.
  • Apart from that, we do not refund partly used periods.

After a refund

Refunding a subscription also cancels it. Your licence key stops working and PinCurate goes back to the free version. Your watchlist and growth history stay in your browser and come back if you get Pro again; saved pins are then kept for the free 7 days.
