Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

PinCurate is a Chrome extension made by Univa Apps ("we"). It keeps what it needs in your browser and does not send your data to us. If you use Pro, your licence key is sent to our payment provider to check it (see Sharing). This policy explains what PinCurate keeps and when anything leaves your browser.

What PinCurate stores

  • Your settings.
  • Pins, board numbers and pinner numbers you have seen on Pinterest: ids, counts, dates, and public board and pinner names and usernames.
  • The websites you add under My sites, so PinCurate can show where your own pins rank in a search.
  • Your watchlist (pin, board and search ids), the saves history of the pins you track, and the search ranks and board numbers recorded for the searches and boards you watch.
  • If you buy Pro: your licence key, the activation id Dodo Payments gives this browser, and the result of the last check.

All of this is kept in your browser's local extension storage. Saved pins and numbers are deleted after the number of days you choose. You can remove everything at any time with "Clear all data" in the settings, or by uninstalling the extension.

What PinCurate does not collect

  • No emails, passwords, cookies, tokens or Pinterest account details.
  • No browsing history outside Pinterest pages.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.

Sharing

We do not receive your data, and PinCurate does not send it to us or sell it to anyone. The only exceptions are:

  • Licence check (Pro only). When you apply your licence key, and about once a day while you use PinCurate, the key and its activation id are sent to our payment provider, Dodo Payments, to confirm that it is valid. Nothing else is sent with it. Chrome asks for your permission before the first check.
  • Payments. When you buy Pro, you pay on Dodo Payments' checkout page. Dodo Payments is the merchant of record and handles your name, email, payment details and tax information under its own privacy policy. We receive what we need to manage your purchase (such as your email and order details) from Dodo Payments.
  • Thank-you page. After you pay, Dodo Payments sends you to our thank-you page with your licence key and email in the page address. The page shows the key in your browser only, does not send it anywhere, and removes it from the address bar right away. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep its usual logs of page requests.

Pinterest

PinCurate is not affiliated with Pinterest. Pinterest's own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of Pinterest.

Children

PinCurate is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
